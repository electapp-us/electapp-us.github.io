    Your answers as a picture

    How to read the grid. Across: how big a role you want government to play in the economy (taxes, wages, health care, business rules). Up and down: how much you want government to limit personal choices (abortion, guns, drugs, policing). These are two separate questions, so you can want a bigger government role in the economy and more personal freedom at the same time.

    Two axes cannot capture everything, and where a dot lands depends on how the statements are framed. For example, we counted limits on guns and on abortion as "more government control." This is a picture of your answers, not a label or a recommendation. The military statements are listed above but not plotted.
